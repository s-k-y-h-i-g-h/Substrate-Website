## What is Substrate?

Substrate is a knowledge base for people who want to run their own infrastructure — LLMs, health tracking, home servers — without relying on closed platforms or opaque recommendations.

Everything here is **evidence-graded**, **reproducible**, and **self-hosted**.

## Why this exists

Most "documentation" in this space is either marketing copy, outdated tutorials, or uncritical product roundups. Substrate is different:

- **No affiliate links.** No sponsored placements. No hidden incentives.
- **Graded evidence.** Every claim cites its source; every recommendation shows its confidence.
- **Built to run.** Not "how to think about X" — actual compose files, configs, and validation steps.
- **Open source.** The site, the tooling, the data — all MIT/Apache-2.0. Fork it, audit it, improve it.
