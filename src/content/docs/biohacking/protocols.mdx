# Supplement Protocols

Each protocol lists the intervention, dose, timing, evidence grade, contraindications, and monitoring markers. Protocols are **not medical advice** — they are structured summaries of the evidence for self-experimentation.

## How to use this

1. **Pick one protocol at a time** — isolate variables
2. **Baseline first** — 2 weeks of tracking before starting
3. **Run 4–8 weeks** — most supplements need accumulation
4. **Track the monitor column** — subjective + objective where possible
5. **Reassess** — continue, adjust, or stop
