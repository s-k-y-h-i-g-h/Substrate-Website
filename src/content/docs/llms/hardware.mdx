# Hardware Guide

The single constraint for local LLM inference is **VRAM**. Everything else (CPU, system RAM, storage speed) is secondary.

## VRAM requirements by model size (4-bit quantization)

| Model | Parameters | VRAM (4-bit) | VRAM (8-bit) | Notes |
|-------|------------|--------------|--------------|-------|
| Llama 3.2 1B | 1B | 1.5 GB | 2.5 GB | Fast, limited capability |
| Llama 3.2 3B | 3B | 3 GB | 5 GB | Solid for coding/summarization |
| Phi-3.5 3.8B | 3.8B | 3.5 GB | 6 GB | Microsoft, strong benchmarks |
| Llama 3.1 8B | 8B | 6 GB | 10 GB | **Sweet spot** — general purpose |
| Qwen 2.5 14B | 14B | 10 GB | 17 GB | Strong multilingual |
| Llama 3.1 70B | 70B | 40 GB | 75 GB | Needs 2× 24 GB or 48 GB card |

> **Rule of thumb:** `VRAM_GB ≈ params_B × 0.75` for 4-bit (GGUF Q4_K_M).

## GPU recommendations (used market, Oct 2026)

| GPU | VRAM | Used price | Best for |
|-----|------|------------|----------|
| RTX 3060 12 GB | 12 GB | ~$200 | 8B–14B Q4, entry point |
| RTX 4060 Ti 16 GB | 16 GB | ~$350 | 14B–32B Q4, best $/VRAM |
| RTX 3090 / 4090 24 GB | 24 GB | $700–$1100 | 70B Q4, 32B Q8, multi-model |
| RTX 6000 Ada 48 GB | 48 GB | $3000+ | 70B Q8, 120B+ models |
| Dual 3090/4090 | 48 GB | $1400–$2200 | 70B Q8, parallel serving |

**Avoid:** 8 GB cards (4060, 3070, 4070) — they only fit 7B Q4, and 8B models are the floor for useful general-purpose work.

## CPU offloading

llama.cpp can offload layers to system RAM when VRAM is exhausted. It works but:
- 10–50× slower per token
- Only viable for batch / background jobs
- Not interactive

If you're buying *for* LLMs, buy VRAM first.

## System RAM

- Minimum: 32 GB (16 GB for model + 16 GB for OS / context)
- Recommended: 64 GB (allows CPU offloading without swapping)

## Storage

- NVMe required for model loading speed
- 1 TB minimum (models are 4–40 GB each)
- Model cache directory should be on the fastest drive
